## 2. Label-quality heuristics

Rules (all on folded text: diacritics removed, hamza/alef/ya/ta-marbuta unified, the same `fold` and clitic `variants` as `tools/find_candidates.py`):

- **span_no_query_term**: the evidence span of a visual association contains no surface form of the target's taxonomy `query_terms_ar` (scene group terms plus the terms of its member tags). `..._extra_only` = the span matches only the extra search terms used by the candidate finder (`EXTRA_SCENE_TERMS`, not part of the taxonomy), e.g. شمس, ريح.
- **literal_after_negation**: a literal span preceded (within 2 words, same hemistich) by لا/لم/ما/ليس/لن/غير/بلا; **literal_in_wish_or_condition**: ليت/لو/لعل earlier in the same hemistich; **literal_after_simile_particle**: كأن/مثل within 2 words (weak: folded كأن equals كان).
- **scene_association_generic**: the justification contains neither a scene term of the target nor any content word (3+ letters) of the verse.
- **context_in_justification**: a visual or emotion justification mentions «السياق», «البيت السابق» … (the label rests on a verse the reader will not see).
- **context_in_meaning**: the AI meaning mentions «القصيدة», «السياق», «السابق», «ما قبله», «يكمل» … («في سياق» alone is ignored: it names a genre, not another verse); **lead_connective** (ثم/حتى/إذ/فإذا/بل/لكن), **lead_pronoun** (هو/هي/هم/ذلك/تلك/الذي …) as first word; **lead_wa_fa** (weak: و/ف may be a root letter); **short_verse** < 30 folded letters; **apcd_text_note**.
- **love_or_praise_in_meaning**: the AI meaning names a ممدوح/محبوب/غزل/خمر (the QA audit's most common emotion error: ghazal or madih treated as the target emotion).
- **city_label_check_heritage**: any `scene_group.city` label (QA rule: pre-modern cities belong to heritage).

## 3. Triage score (0–14)

Seven components, 0–2 each. A **triage heuristic for ordering review, not a measure of label quality**:

| Component | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| meaning | meaning_ar ≥ 10 words, no context marker | shorter, or mentions poem context | absent |
| standalone | no flag | only lead_wa_fa | context_in_meaning, context_in_justification, lead_connective, lead_pronoun, short_verse or missing hemistich (an APCD text_note counts only under text_attribution) |
| evidence (best tier-1 scene relation for the cell) | literal span containing a taxonomy scene term | literal span without a term, or specific scene_association | generic scene_association, or no tier-1 relation |
| scene_link | literal with no cue | literal after simile particle, or a city label | literal after negation / inside wish, generic scene_association, none; scene_association = 1 |
| emotion_link | emotion accepted by human:renad, or expressed with span and low uncertainty | evoked/low or expressed/medium | high uncertainty, or further −1 for love/praise marker |
| text_attribution | source_verified, no text_note | apcd_only, no text_note | text_note or disputed |
| rights | public_domain_classical / public_domain_all | – | us_risk / unverified |

"Strong" candidate for a cell = total ≥ 11, evidence ≥ 1, scene_link ≥ 1, standalone ≥ 1, rights 2, text_attribution ≥ 1. Histogram of totals (best scene group per record): 6: 3, 7: 12, 8: 35, 9: 100, 10: 206, 11: 300, 12: 279, 13: 118. Strong records: 659 of 1,053.

## 5. Limits

- Heuristics are lexical. Substring-free token matching misses broken plurals not in the term list and may accept a homograph; negation/wish cues ignore scope. Counts are flags, not errors.
- Person and place names that need the poem (e.g. a patron's name) are only caught when the AI meaning mentions a ممدوح or the poem context.
- The score treats the AI's own `annotator_uncertainty` as a signal, which is self-reported by the drafting model.
